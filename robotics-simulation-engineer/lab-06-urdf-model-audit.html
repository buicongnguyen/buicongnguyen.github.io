Step 4

Inspect solver ownership in the Stage

  1. Find the intended ArticulationRootAPI owner.
  2. Enable Physics → Colliders → All; compare collision and visual geometry.
  3. Check every joint axis, origin, limit, drive target, and wheel velocity drive.
  4. Check mass, center of mass, inertia orientation, and units.
  5. Use Physics Inspector; verify no nominal-pose collider intersection.
