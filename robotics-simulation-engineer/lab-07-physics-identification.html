Step 1

Freeze inputs and predeclare outputs

Inputs

stage hash, pose/reset, timestep, command, build

Metrics

stop distance, yaw error, settling time, wheel response

Threshold

improvement must exceed repeat variability

Contact ownership: record both materials and their friction, restitution, compliant-contact, and combine modes.

Step 2

Measure the noise floor first

  1. Run the identical baseline at least five times.
  2. Compute mean, standard deviation, min, and max.
  3. Separate simulation-stamp metrics from wall-arrival metrics.
  4. If variation exceeds the expected improvement, repair reset/timing before tuning.
