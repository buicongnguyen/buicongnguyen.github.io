Step 3

Use the Isaac Sim 6.0 source → publisher pattern

New graphs separate data acquisition from ROS publication. Do not copy deprecated direct-prim wiring from older tutorials.

Joint state

Isaac Read Joint State → ROS2 Publish Joint State

Connect execution, names, position, velocity, effort, DOF types, scale, and sensor time.

Transform tree

Isaac Compute Transform Tree → ROS2 Publish Transform Tree

Connect execution, parent/child frames, translations, and orientations.

Odometry

Isaac Compute Odometry → ROS2 Publish Odometry

Declare chassis, odom, base_link, pose, and full 3D velocity.
Prim-path rule: robot-wide state graphs belong at the robot root. NVIDIA documents /World/tb3_burger_processed for the shipped scene; verify your actual Stage instead of blindly copying it.

Step 4

Inspect type, sample, publisher, and rate

pwsh -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File $Launcher ros2 topic list -t

Collect at least 20 samples for each rate. Topic existence is discovery evidence; sample structure and frame/time behavior are the gate.
